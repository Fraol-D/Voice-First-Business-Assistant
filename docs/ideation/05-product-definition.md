# Product Definition

## Proposed product

A **voice-first operating assistant for microbusinesses** that:

- converts spoken business activity into **validated business events**;
- maintains **persistent business state**;
- **visualizes** that state;
- and provides **data-grounded operational decision support**.

Working title: Voice-First Business Assistant.  
Tagline: Run your business by voice.

None of these components is implemented yet. This file defines intent.

## Who it is for

Owner-operated microbusinesses that already produce a daily stream of sales, purchases, expenses, stock movements, and customer credit — and that currently keep that stream in memory, paper, or tools they underuse.

The definition does not assume a single country, language, or sector. Research that motivated the problem is concentrated in African microenterprise and SMB settings; the first implementation may need a narrower beachhead, which is not chosen here.

## Intended distinction of layers

| Layer | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice** | Interaction layer. The owner speaks activity and questions. |
| **Structured business events** | Data layer. A sale, expense, purchase, payment, or inventory movement is a typed, validated record — not a chat transcript. |
| **Persistent business state** | Source of truth. Products, customers, quantities, balances, and receivables are stored and updated from events. |
| **Deterministic calculations** | Numerical metrics (totals, stock on hand, amounts owed) are computed from state and events, not generated by a model. |
| **AI** | Interprets natural language, maps speech to candidate events or queries, and **explains** results. It does not own the numbers. |
| **Visualization** | Makes business state understandable without requiring the owner to read a ledger. |

## Intended core flow

Voice → Validated Business Event → Business State → Analysis → Decision

Spoken questions follow the same rule: interpret the question, read state, calculate, then explain.

## Planned capabilities (not built)

- Voice-based business activity capture
- Sales and expense tracking
- Inventory tracking
- Customer receivables
- Business dashboard
- Business visualizations
- Voice-based business queries
- Operational decision support

## Out of scope for this definition

- Claiming that features already work
- Treating the LLM as a general-purpose accountant
- Replacing the need for the owner to confirm ambiguous events
- A full accounting suite, payroll, tax filing, or payments product
